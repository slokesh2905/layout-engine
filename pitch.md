# Adaptive Layout Studio
### One ad. Every screen. Zero guesswork.

---

## The Problem

You design a beautiful ad. It looks perfect on a phone. Then it gets placed on a kiosk, a broadcast ticker, a tablet in landscape — and it falls apart. Text gets cropped. The logo disappears. The call-to-action button is nowhere to be seen.

Today, fixing this means your designers manually rebuild the same ad for every screen size. Every time something changes — a new headline, a different product image, a price update — it has to be redone. Across every format. By hand.

That's not a workflow. That's a tax on creativity.

---

## What Adaptive Layout Studio Does

**Adaptive Layout Studio** is an intelligent ad composition tool that takes your ad — your content, your brand, your message — and **automatically figures out the best possible layout for any screen**, in real time.

You define your ad once. The engine handles the rest.

It knows your headline matters more than your logo. It knows a CTA button should never disappear. It knows what to shrink, what to trim, and — only as a last resort — what to drop, so the ad still makes sense and still converts, even on the tightest screens.

---

## How It Works (In Plain English)

Think of your ad as a set of ingredients, each with a **priority**. Your headline and hero image? Top priority — never touched. Your supporting copy? Can be trimmed a little if space is tight. Your logo? Important, but the ad works without it if something has to give.

You tell the studio what the ingredients are and how much each one matters. The engine takes it from there — placing every element at the right size, in the right position, for whatever screen it's on.

No manual tweaking. No breakpoints to manage. No pixel-pushing for each format.

## What You Can Do With It

### ✦ Build ads from scratch
Start with a blank slate. Add your headline, your image, your CTA. Set priorities. Hit go.

### ✦ Preview across every surface instantly
Switch between **Mobile Portrait, Mobile Landscape, Square Kiosk, Broadcast Lower Third, Compact Badge** — or type in any custom dimensions — and watch the layout adapt live. No refresh, no rebuild.

### ✦ See exactly what the engine decided — and why
The **Elements panel** shows you every element: whether it was placed perfectly, whether it was shrunk to fit, whether it was trimmed at the edges, or whether it was dropped entirely. The **Degradation Order** tells you upfront, before you even preview, in what order the engine will make sacrifices if space gets tight.

### ✦ Drag to reprioritize, live
Not happy with the engine's choices? Drag an element up or down the priority list and watch the layout re-resolve instantly. You're not overriding the engine — you're giving it better instructions, and it responds immediately.

### ✦ Edit content without leaving the canvas
Change a headline, update a button label, swap a product image — all inline, all live. What you see is what gets exported.

### ✦ Export for everything
- **Export Spec** — the editable source file you can reopen, share, or hand to a colleague.
- **Export PNG** — a pixel-perfect render, ready to drop into a campaign.
- **Export SVG** — drag it straight into Figma and get real, editable layers, not a flat image.
- **Export Layout JSON** — for engineers who want the exact positions, sizes, and decisions for programmatic rendering.
- **Export All Surfaces** — one click, every format, as a full set of JSON + PNG pairs. Done.

### ✦ Import from anywhere
- Import a spec file you exported before.
- Paste a spec directly from a chat or email — no file needed.
- Import a Figma frame — export your SVG from Figma, import it here, and it becomes a living spec you can re-resolve across any surface.

### ✦ Works entirely in your browser
No install. No backend. No account. Your content never leaves your machine. The engine runs entirely client-side — which means it's instant, private, and works offline.
